Robustness evaluation

Run paired watermarked and unwatermarked generations, measure detection and false positives, then apply synthetic deletion and substitution attacks. Larger runs take longer on CPU.

Uses the gamma, delta and z-score threshold above. Attacks: unchanged control, word deletion, word substitution and lexical paraphrase proxy.

Watermark compatibility

The Kirchenbauer method needs generation-time token logits and a compatible tokenizer. Hugging Face causal language models are the primary embedding route. Standard chat APIs and Ollama's ordinary generate endpoint are listed for comparison or paraphrasing, not as watermark-capable by default.
